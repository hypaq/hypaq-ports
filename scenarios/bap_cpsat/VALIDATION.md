## Limits

The new notebook was not launched in a full Jupyter/Colab kernel session.
No GitHub upload, remote workflow or release publication was performed.
Other operating systems and architectures were not validated for this delivery.

These are tiny synthetic one-slot BAP exercises. They do not establish quantum
speedup, equal-resource algorithm rankings, general BAP scalability or industrial
readiness. Timers have different scopes. The unchanged quantum runners perform
their own tiny exact-reference preparation; the CP-SAT adapter does not call
enumeration. Existing notebooks and archived results are not regenerated by this
scenario. Dependency pins cover direct dependencies, not a complete lockfile.
